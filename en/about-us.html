About Us

Who we are

We are an association of young people who support each other. We came together to look out for one another, share what we know and build things together.

Our story

We were born from something very simple: a group of young people who decided not to leave anyone behind. We started by getting together to talk about what was on our minds, and little by little that space became an association.

Today we keep the same idea: that supporting each other should feel natural, that asking for help should never be embarrassing, and that what one person learns should also help the rest.

«Jóvenes que se apoyan entre sí»

Our mission

To stand by young people who need support, creating safe spaces for listening, learning and mutual aid where no one feels alone.

Our vision

A community where supporting each other is the norm: young people holding each other up, with a network strong enough for anyone to find help when they need it.

Our values

These are the principles that guide everything we do:

  • Mutual support

    We look after each other as equals: asking for help and offering it is part of who we are.

  • Closeness

    We speak plainly, without jargon or judgement, and we address every person by name.

  • Commitment

    What we say we will do, we do. With consistency and with time.

  • Transparency

    We publish who we are and where every resource we manage comes from.
