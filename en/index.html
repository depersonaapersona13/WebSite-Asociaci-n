What we do

Real support, peer to peer

We create spaces where young people meet, listen to each other and support one another without feeling judged.

  • Mutual support

    We walk alongside those who need it: listening, guidance and practical help between people of similar ages.

  • Meet-ups and activities

    We organise gatherings, workshops and activities to get to know each other, learn together and switch off.

  • Community

    We build a network: what one person learns is shared with the rest, and nobody is left out.

We answer your questions

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before reaching out or joining our meetups.

Any young person who wants to feel heard, support others, or propose community activities. There are no prerequisites: our doors are open to everyone.

None at all. Taking part in the association and our meetups is 100% free. We believe in accessible peer support without economic barriers.

We organise informal meetups, spaces for conversation and listening, practical workshops, group sessions to share concerns, and solidarity projects suggested by the members themselves.

The association was founded in Seville and we hold in-person meetups there, but we also have online channels and digital activities so you can join from anywhere.

It's very easy! You can write to us through the contact form on this website or send a direct message on any of our social networks. We'll be glad to hear about your idea.

Do you want to take part?

If you're young and want to support others or feel supported yourself, write to us. You can also count on us if you'd like to suggest an activity or collaborate with the association.
